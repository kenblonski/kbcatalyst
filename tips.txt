# Kettlebell Catalyst: form tips, one per line.
# Lines starting with # are ignored. Blank lines are fine.
Swings: the hips snap, the bell floats. Your arms are just ropes.
Swings: hike the bell high between your thighs, like passing a football.
Swings: at the top, stand tall. Glutes tight, knees locked, ribs down.
Get-ups: eyes on the bell, arm locked out, the whole way.
Get-ups: slow is smooth, smooth is strong.
Presses: squeeze the handle, brace your abs, then press.
Presses: wrist straight. The bell rests on your forearm, not your hand.
Rack: elbow tucked, bell in the crook of your arm, wrist neutral.
Squats: sit between your heels, chest up, knees out.
Deadlifts: push the floor away. Flat back, bell close.
Rows: pull to the hip, not the chest.
Carries: walk tall, short steps, don't lean.
Cleans: the bell travels close to the body. No banging the forearm.
Snatches: punch through at the top so the bell doesn't flip over.
Windmills: hips back, eyes on the bell, go only as far as is comfortable.
Lunges: step long enough that the front knee stays over the ankle.
Planks: squeeze glutes and quads. A tight body is a safe body.
Breathe out sharply on each swing, like a short hiss.
Grip fading? Shake it out and reset. Form first.
If a rep feels sloppy, park it. Rest out the interval.
Feet flat, toes gripping the floor. Root down.
Neck long, gaze a few feet ahead on hinges.
Heavier soon? Only when every rep of every set looks clean.
Pick the bell up and set it down with a flat back, every time.
Sip water between sets if you need to. No rush.
Chalk or a towel helps if your hands sweat.
Wear flat shoes, or go barefoot on a safe floor.
Leave room around you. Swings travel further than you think.
Sore today? Make it an easy day and keep the chain.
Finish every set with a rep you're proud of.
